Connect this device

Your numbers live in a private GitHub repository, so this page needs a token before it can read them. One token works on all your devices; paste it once on each.

  1. Open GitHub's token page. The name, a one-year expiry and the Contents permission are filled in.
  2. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick money-plan-data.
  3. Click Generate token, copy it and paste it below.

The token can only read and write that one private repository. You can revoke it any time in GitHub's settings. On an iPhone, add this page to your Home Screen so Safari doesn't forget the token.

How to think about money

House rules

Pay yourself firstSavings leave the day after payday, for an account you can't see from your phone.
Every pound has a jobPlan the month before it starts. What's left goes to savings, not the next online basket.
One-offs aren't one-offsMost months have one. Fund them on purpose through the irregular pot.
No credit for things that lose valueNone at all while you're clearing debt, and that includes pay-later and device plans.
Raises and bonuses go to goalsKeep spending where it is when your pay goes up. The gap is what builds money.
The cheapest car is the one you ownEvery month without a car payment funds the next car, or the next move.
